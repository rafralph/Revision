ONE HOUR A DAY

Homework planner

Tell the planner what homework you have. Each day it makes a one-hour plan: three 15-minute homework pieces first, then a 15-minute revision session on one subject. Homework due soonest goes first. Revision takes turns between subjects and picks the most important topic you have not revised yet.

1. Add your homework

Adding the topic helps the planner find the right guides on this site, so you know where to look if you get stuck. Longer homework is split into 15-minute pieces and spread over the days before it is due.

2. Your plan

Tick each piece when you finish it. Ticking homework updates its progress; ticking revision records the topic so it is not picked again too soon. Stand up and stretch for a minute between pieces.
