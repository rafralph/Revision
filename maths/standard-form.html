Standard form

Explanations 4 ways

1The picture idea

Each power of ten is one place on a ladder. Multiplying by 10ⁿ moves the decimal point n places. Multiplying powers of ten just adds the steps.

2Step by step

Standard form writes a number as A × 10ⁿ, where 1 ≤ A < 10 and n is a whole number. AQA tests converting, ordering and calculating with standard form, often without a calculator.

  1. Multiply or divide the front numbers.
  2. For multiplication, add the powers of ten. For division, subtract them.
  3. Check the front number. It must be at least 1 and less than 10.
  4. If it is too big, divide it by 10 and add 1 to the power. If it is too small, multiply it by 10 and subtract 1 from the power.

CAUTION15 × 10⁻³ is not in standard form. Write it as 1.5 × 10⁻².

3Another way to see it

Standard form is a short way to write very big or very small numbers: a × 10ⁿ, where a is at least 1 and less than 10.

  • Big number → positive power. 45 000 = 4.5 × 10⁴ (the point jumps 4 places).
  • Small number → negative power. 0.0072 = 7.2 × 10⁻³.

To multiply: multiply the front numbers, add the powers. To divide: divide the front numbers, subtract the powers. Then fix the front number so it is between 1 and 10.

To add or subtract, change to ordinary numbers first (or make the powers the same).

4Watch out for

  • Leaving the front number outside 1 to 10, e.g. 15 × 10⁻³.
  • Multiplying the powers instead of adding them.
  • Getting the sign of the power wrong for small numbers.
  • Adding the powers when you add two numbers.
  • Typing it into a calculator wrongly. Use the ×10ˣ button.

Worked examples 3

Example 1 Warm-up

Write 45 000 in standard form.

  1. 4.5 and the point moves 4 places

Answer: 4.5 × 10⁴

Example 2 Standard

Work out (3 × 10⁴) × (5 × 10⁻⁷). Give the answer in standard form.

  1. 3 × 5 = 15; 10⁴⁺⁽⁻⁷⁾ = 10⁻³
  2. 15 × 10⁻³ = 1.5 × 10⁻²

Answer: 1.5 × 10⁻²

Example 3 Exam-style [4 marks]

Work out (a) (3 × 10⁴) × (5 × 10⁻⁷) (b) (6 × 10⁵) ÷ (4 × 10⁻²). Give your answers in standard form.

  1. (a) 3 × 5 = 15 ; 10⁴ × 10⁻⁷ = 10⁻³ → 15 × 10⁻³ = 1.5 × 10⁻²
  2. (b) 6 ÷ 4 = 1.5 ; 10⁵ ÷ 10⁻² = 10⁵⁻⁽⁻²⁾ = 10⁷

Answer: (a) 1.5 × 10⁻² (b) 1.5 × 10⁷
