Equation of a circle and tangents

Rank 31 of 36 · 25 marks · 1.3% of all marks · in 8 of 24 papers

Explanations 4 ways

1The picture idea

Every point on a circle is the same distance r from the centre. Pythagoras on that distance gives x² + y² = r².

2Step by step

At GCSE, the circle has its centre at the origin. Its equation is x² + y² = r². AQA tests reading the radius, checking if a point is on the circle, and finding the equation of a tangent.

  1. The radius is the square root of the number on the right. x² + y² = 25 has radius 5.
  2. Find the gradient of the radius to the point: y ÷ x.
  3. The tangent is perpendicular to the radius. Its gradient is the negative reciprocal.
  4. Put the gradient and the point into y − y₁ = m(x − x₁), or into y = mx + c.
  5. Simplify to the form the question asks for.

CAUTIONx² + y² = 25 has radius 5, not 25.

3Another way to see it

x² + y² = r² is just Pythagoras. Any point (x, y) on the circle is r away from the centre, so x² + y² = r². The circle x² + y² = 25 has radius √25 = 5.

A tangent touches the circle at one point and meets the radius at 90°. So:

  1. Find the gradient of the radius: y ÷ x.
  2. Flip it and change the sign (negative reciprocal).
  3. Use y − y₁ = m(x − x₁) with the point.

4Watch out for

  • Saying x² + y² = 25 has radius 25. The radius is √25 = 5.
  • Using the radius gradient for the tangent. The tangent is perpendicular.
  • Forgetting to change the sign when you flip the gradient.
  • Sign errors with negative coordinates in y − y₁ = m(x − x₁).
  • Not giving the final equation in the form the question asks for.

Worked examples 3

Example 1 Warm-up

Write the radius of the circle x² + y² = 49.

  1. r² = 49
  2. r = √49

Answer: 7

Example 2 Standard

Find the equation of the tangent to x² + y² = 25 at (3, 4).

  1. Radius gradient = 4/3
  2. Tangent gradient = −3/4
  3. y − 4 = −¾(x − 3)

Answer: y = −¾x + 25/4

Example 3 Exam-style [4 marks]

The circle x² + y² = 25 passes through P(3, 4). Find the equation of the tangent to the circle at P.

  1. Gradient of radius OP = 4 ÷ 3
  2. Gradient of tangent = −34 (negative reciprocal)
  3. y − 4 = −34(x − 3)
  4. y = −34x + 94 + 4 = −34x + 254

Answer: y = −¾x + 25/4 (or 3x + 4y = 25)

Q5Exam-style

Find the equation of the tangent to x² + y² = 100 at (−6, 8).

Show answer
  1. Radius gradient = 8 ÷ −6 = −4/3
  2. Tangent gradient = ¾
  3. y − 8 = ¾(x + 6)

Answer: y = ¾x + 12.5

Q6Exam-style

The tangent to x² + y² = 5 at (1, 2) crosses the x-axis at P. Find P.

Show answer
  1. Radius gradient 2 → tangent −½
  2. y − 2 = −½(x − 1) → y = −½x + 5/2
  3. y = 0 → x = 5

Answer: P = (5, 0)
