Non-linear graphs and graph transformations

Rank 20 of 36 · 50 marks · 2.6% of all marks · in 17 of 24 papers

VideoExplanationsDiagramsWorked examplesPractice

Explanations 4 ways

1The picture idea

Outside the bracket changes y, the way you expect. Inside the bracket changes x, the opposite way. f(x + 4) reaches each value 4 units early, so it moves left.

2Step by step

Non-linear graphs include quadratics, cubics, reciprocals (y = 1/x), exponentials and trig graphs. AQA asks you to recognise their shapes, read solutions from them and transform them.

  1. Find a key point on y = f(x), for example the turning point.
  2. For y = f(x) + a, move the graph up by a. Add a to each y-coordinate.
  3. For y = f(x + a), move the graph left by a. Subtract a from each x-coordinate.
  4. For y = −f(x), reflect in the x-axis. Change the sign of each y-coordinate.
  5. For y = f(−x), reflect in the y-axis. Change the sign of each x-coordinate.

CAUTIONf(x + 4) moves left, not right. f(x − 4) moves right.

3Another way to see it

One rule to remember: outside the bracket changes y and does what it says. Inside the bracket changes x and does the opposite.

  • f(x) + 3: up 3 (outside, does what it says).
  • f(x + 3): left 3 (inside, opposite).
  • −f(x): flip upside down (reflect in the x-axis).
  • f(−x): flip left to right (reflect in the y-axis).

Follow one point, like the turning point, and move it.

4Watch out for

  • Moving f(x + 2) to the right. It moves left by 2.
  • Mixing up −f(x) (y-values change sign) and f(−x) (x-values change sign).
  • Moving the point in both directions at once.
  • Describing a translation without a vector.
  • Forgetting to complete the square to find the translation for a quadratic.

Worked examples 3

Example 1 Warm-up

y = f(x) has a turning point at (2, −3). Write the turning point of y = f(x) + 4.

  1. Outside the bracket: up 4

Answer: (2, 1)

Example 2 Standard

Write the turning point of y = f(x − 3).

  1. Inside the bracket: opposite, so right 3

Answer: (5, −3)

Example 3 Exam-style [3 marks]

The graph of y = f(x) has a turning point at (2, −3). Write down the turning point of (a) y = f(x) + 5, (b) y = f(x + 4), (c) y = −f(x).

  1. (a) Up 5: (2, −3 + 5)
  2. (b) Left 4: (2 − 4, −3)
  3. (c) Reflect in the x-axis: (2, 3)

Answer: (a) (2, 2) (b) (−2, −3) (c) (2, 3)
