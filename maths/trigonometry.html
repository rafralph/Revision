Sine rule, cosine rule and trig graphs

Explanations 4 ways

1The picture idea

The cosine rule is Pythagoras with a correction. At 90°, cos C = 0 and the correction disappears. Close the angle and the third side shrinks.

2Step by step

These rules work in any triangle, not only right-angled triangles. Label the sides a, b, c and the opposite angles A, B, C. AQA also tests the graphs of sin, cos and tan and exact values such as sin 30° = ½.

  1. Look at what you know.
  2. If you know two sides and the angle between them, use the cosine rule: c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C.
  3. If you know three sides and need an angle, rearrange the cosine rule: cos C = (a² + b² − c²) ÷ 2ab.
  4. If you know a side and its opposite angle, use the sine rule: a ÷ sin A = b ÷ sin B.
  5. For the area, use ½ab sin C.

CAUTIONSet your calculator to degrees. Do the 2ab cos C part before you subtract.

3Another way to see it

Look for a pair: an angle and the side opposite it, where you know both.

  • You have a full pair (plus one more fact) → sine rule: a/sin A = b/sin B.
  • No full pair (two sides and the angle between, or three sides) → cosine rule: a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos A.
  • Area with two sides and the angle between them → ½ab sin C.

Label first: small letter a is the side opposite capital angle A.

4Watch out for

  • Using Pythagoras or SOHCAHTOA on a triangle with no right angle.
  • Mislabelling: side a must be opposite angle A.
  • Working out b² + c² − 2bc first, then multiplying by cos A. Use the calculator in one go, or follow BIDMAS.
  • Forgetting to square-root at the end of the cosine rule.
  • Calculator in radians instead of degrees.

Worked examples 3

Example 1 Warm-up

Work out the area of a triangle with sides 5 cm and 8 cm and angle 30° between them.

  1. Area = ½ × 5 × 8 × sin 30°
  2. = 20 × 0.5

Answer: 10 cm²

Example 2 Standard

In triangle ABC, A = 40°, B = 65° and a = 7 cm. Work out b.

  1. Pair: a and A → sine rule
  2. b = 7 × sin 65° ÷ sin 40° = 9.869…

Answer: 9.87 cm

Example 3 Exam-style [5 marks]

In triangle ABC, a = 7 cm, b = 9 cm and angle C = 50°. (a) Work out the length c. (b) Work out the area of the triangle.

  1. (a) c² = 7² + 9² − 2 × 7 × 9 × cos 50° = 130 − 80.99 = 49.01
  2. c = √49.01 = 7.0006…
  3. (b) Area = 12 × 7 × 9 × sin 50° = 24.13…

Answer: (a) 7.00 cm (b) 24.1 cm²

  1. ½ × 6 × 9 × sin 50° = 20.68…

Q3Standard

a = 8 cm, b = 11 cm and C = 40°. Work out c.

Show answer
  1. c² = 64 + 121 − 2 × 8 × 11 × cos 40°
  2. c² = 185 − 134.82 = 50.18

Answer: c = 7.08 cm

Q4Standard

A triangle has sides 5 cm, 6 cm and 7 cm. Work out the largest angle.

Show answer
  1. Largest angle is opposite 7
  2. cos θ = (25 + 36 − 49) ÷ (2 × 5 × 6) = 12 ÷ 60 = 0.2

Answer: 78.5°

Q5Exam-style

a = 10 cm, A = 50° and b = 12 cm. Work out angle B (acute).

Show answer
  1. sin B = 12 × sin 50° ÷ 10 = 0.9193
  2. B = sin⁻¹(0.9193)

Answer: 66.8°

Q6Exam-style

Solve sin x = 0.5 for 0° ≤ x ≤ 360°.

Show answer
  1. sin⁻¹(0.5) = 30°
  2. The sine graph is symmetrical about 90°: 180° − 30° = 150°

Answer: x = 30° or 150°
