Factors, multiples and primes

Explanations 4 ways

1The picture idea

Primes are the building blocks of whole numbers. Put both numbers' primes in a Venn diagram: the overlap is the HCF, and everything together is the LCM.

2Step by step

A prime number has exactly two factors: 1 and itself. AQA tests prime factor decomposition, highest common factor (HCF) and lowest common multiple (LCM), often in a word problem.

  1. Write each number as a product of primes. Use a factor tree.
  2. Write the answer in index form, for example 2³ × 3² × 5.
  3. Draw a Venn diagram. Put the shared primes in the middle.
  4. The HCF is the product of the primes in the middle.
  5. The LCM is the product of all the primes in the diagram.

CAUTION1 is not a prime number. Do not put it in a factor tree.

3Another way to see it

Every whole number is built from primes, like a unique barcode. A factor tree splits the number into two factors, again and again, until every branch ends in a prime.

Put the two numbers' prime factors into a Venn diagram. Shared primes go in the middle.

  • HCF (highest common factor) = multiply the middle only.
  • LCM (lowest common multiple) = multiply everything in the diagram.

Real-life clue: "when will they next happen together?" → LCM. "Biggest equal groups?" → HCF.

4Watch out for

  • Thinking 1 is prime. It is not: a prime has exactly two factors.
  • Mixing up HCF and LCM.
  • Reading 3² as 6. It means 3 × 3 = 9.
  • Stopping the factor tree before every branch is prime.
  • Forgetting a repeated prime in the Venn diagram (e.g. both numbers have two 2s).

Worked examples 3

Example 1 Warm-up

Write 60 as a product of prime factors in index form.

  1. 60 = 2 × 30 = 2 × 2 × 15 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5

Answer: 2² × 3 × 5

Example 2 Standard

Find the HCF and LCM of 12 and 18.

  1. 12 = 2² × 3; 18 = 2 × 3²
  2. Shared: 2 × 3 = 6
  3. All: 2² × 3² = 36

Answer: HCF 6, LCM 36

Example 3 Exam-style [5 marks]

(a) Write 360 and 84 as products of prime factors. (b) Find the highest common factor of 360 and 84. (c) Find the lowest common multiple of 360 and 84.

  1. (a) 360 = 2³ × 3² × 5 ; 84 = 2² × 3 × 7
  2. (b) Shared primes: 2 × 2 × 3
  3. (c) All primes: 2³ × 3² × 5 × 7 = 8 × 9 × 5 × 7

Answer: (b) 12 (c) 2520
