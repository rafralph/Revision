Constructions, loci and bearings

Explanations 4 ways

1The picture idea

A bearing is a compass turn: start facing north and turn clockwise. To come back, turn round: add or subtract 180°.

2Step by step

A bearing gives a direction as an angle clockwise from north, with three figures. AQA also tests scale drawings, perpendicular bisectors, angle bisectors and loci.

  1. Draw a north line at the point where you start.
  2. Measure the angle clockwise from north.
  3. Write the bearing with three figures, for example 060°.
  4. For the back bearing, add 180°. If the result is more than 360°, subtract 180° instead.
  5. For constructions, use compasses. Do not rub out the construction arcs.

CAUTION"The bearing of A from B" means stand at B and look at A.

3Another way to see it

Three rules for bearings:

  1. Start at north.
  2. Turn clockwise.
  3. Write 3 figures: 45° is written 045°.

"The bearing of B from A" means stand at A, face north, and turn to face B. The word after "from" is where you stand.

The back bearing (A from B) is the opposite way: add 180°, or subtract 180° if the answer would be over 360°.

Bearing questions often need Pythagoras or trigonometry with a right-angled triangle.

4Watch out for

  • Measuring anticlockwise or from east.
  • Writing 45° instead of 045°.
  • Standing at the wrong point. Draw north at the point after 'from'.
  • Giving a back bearing over 360°.
  • Forgetting that north lines at two points are parallel (use alternate or co-interior angles).

Worked examples 3

Example 1 Warm-up

Write the bearing of due east.

  1. Quarter turn clockwise from north

Answer: 090°

Example 2 Standard

The bearing of B from A is 060°. Work out the bearing of A from B.

  1. 060 + 180

Answer: 240°

Example 3 Exam-style [3 marks]

A ship sails 8 km from A to B on a bearing of 060°. (a) Write down the bearing of A from B. (b) Work out how far east of A the ship is.

  1. (a) Back bearing = 60° + 180°
  2. (b) East distance = 8 × sin 60°
  3. = 6.928… km

Answer: (a) 240° (b) 6.93 km

Practice 6 questions · easy to exam-style

Q1Warm-up

Write the bearing of south-west.

Show answer
  1. 180 + 45

Answer: 225°

Q2Warm-up

Write the bearing of due west.

Show answer
  1. Three quarter turns clockwise

Answer: 270°

Q3Standard

The bearing of Q from P is 310°. Work out the bearing of P from Q.

Show answer
  1. 310 − 180 (adding would go over 360)

Answer: 130°

Q4Standard

The bearing of A from B is 125°. Work out the bearing of B from A.

Show answer
  1. 125 + 180

Answer: 305°

Q5Exam-style

A plane flies 10 km on a bearing of 150°. How far south has it travelled? Give your answer to 1 d.p.

Show answer
  1. The angle between the path and south is 180° − 150° = 30°
  2. South = 10 × cos 30° = 8.660…

Answer: 8.7 km

Q6Exam-style

Jo walks 5 km north, then 12 km east. Work out her distance from the start and the bearing she must walk to get back.

Show answer
  1. Distance = √(5² + 12²) = 13 km
  2. Bearing out = tan⁻¹(12 ÷ 5) = 67.4°
  3. Back bearing = 67.4 + 180

Answer: 13 km on a bearing of 247.4°
