Angles and polygons

Explanations 4 ways

1The picture idea

Walk round any polygon. You turn at each corner and finish facing the way you started. So the turns (exterior angles) add up to one full turn, 360°.

2Step by step

AQA tests angle facts with reasons: angles on a straight line, around a point, in a triangle, and with parallel lines. It also tests interior and exterior angles of polygons.

  1. Exterior angles of any polygon add up to 360°.
  2. For a regular polygon, each exterior angle = 360° ÷ number of sides.
  3. Interior angle + exterior angle = 180°.
  4. The interior angles of an n-sided polygon add up to (n − 2) × 180°.
  5. With parallel lines, look for alternate angles (Z shape) and corresponding angles (F shape).

CAUTIONWrite "alternate angles", not "Z angles". Exam reasons must use the correct names.

3Another way to see it

Triangles inside: from one corner, draw lines to every other corner. A shape with n sides splits into (n − 2) triangles, so the interior angles add to (n − 2) × 180°.

Walk around it: walk along each side and turn at each corner. When you get back to the start, you have turned one full circle. So the exterior angles of any polygon add to 360°.

At each corner, interior + exterior = 180° (a straight line). For a regular polygon, the quickest route is: exterior = 360 ÷ n, then interior = 180 − exterior.

4Watch out for

  • Miscounting the number of sides.
  • Using n × 180 instead of (n − 2) × 180.
  • Assuming all angles are equal in an irregular polygon.
  • Dividing the interior sum by the wrong number of angles.
  • Forgetting that the number of sides must be a whole number.

Worked examples 3

Example 1 Warm-up

Work out the sum of the interior angles of a pentagon.

  1. (5 − 2) × 180

Answer: 540°

Example 2 Standard

Work out the size of each interior angle of a regular octagon.

  1. Exterior = 360 ÷ 8 = 45°
  2. Interior = 180 − 45

Answer: 135°

Example 3 Exam-style [3 marks]

Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 156°. Work out the number of sides.

  1. Exterior angle = 180° − 156° = 24°
  2. Exterior angles add up to 360°
  3. Number of sides = 360 ÷ 24

Answer: 15 sides

  1. Sum = 540°

Can a regular polygon have an interior angle of 130°? Explain.

Show answer
  1. Exterior = 50°
  2. 360 ÷ 50 = 7.2, not a whole number

Answer: No: 7.2 sides is impossible
