Averages and grouped data

Rank 12 of 36 · 62 marks · 3.2% of all marks · in 13 of 24 papers

Explanations 4 ways

1The picture idea

The mean is the balance point. Put each value on a number line as a weight. The mean is where a pivot holds the line level.

2Step by step

An average is one value that represents a set of data. AQA tests the mean, median, mode and range, and estimates from grouped tables. Many questions ask you to compare two groups.

To estimate the mean from a grouped table:

  1. Find the midpoint of each class.
  2. Multiply each midpoint by its frequency.
  3. Add these results.
  4. Divide by the total frequency.

To compare two sets of data, compare an average and compare a spread (range or IQR). Write each comparison in the context of the question.

CAUTIONDivide by the total frequency. Do not divide by the number of classes.

3Another way to see it

  • Mean: share everything out equally. Total ÷ how many.
  • Median: line everyone up in order. The person in the middle.
  • Mode: the most popular value.
  • Range: biggest − smallest. It measures spread, not average.

In a grouped table you do not know the exact values, so pretend everyone in a group is at its midpoint. That is why the answer is an estimate.

Add an extra column: midpoint × frequency. Total that column, then divide by the total frequency.

4Watch out for

  • Dividing by the number of rows instead of the total frequency.
  • Forgetting to put the data in order before finding the median.
  • Using the class width or the upper boundary instead of the midpoint.
  • Calling the grouped mean exact. It is an estimate.
  • Finding the median class by looking for the biggest frequency (that is the modal class).

Worked examples 3

Example 1 Warm-up

Work out the mean of 4, 7, 9, 10, 15.

  1. Total = 45
  2. 45 ÷ 5

Answer: 9

Example 2 Standard

Goals per match: 0 goals 3 times, 1 goal 5 times, 2 goals 4 times, 3 goals 2 times. Work out the mean.

  1. Total goals = 0 + 5 + 8 + 6 = 19
  2. Matches = 14
  3. 19 ÷ 14 = 1.357…

Answer: 1.36 goals (to 2 d.p.)

Example 3 Exam-style [3 marks]

The heights of 20 plants are grouped. 0 < h ≤ 10: 4 plants. 10 < h ≤ 20: 10 plants. 20 < h ≤ 30: 6 plants. Work out an estimate for the mean height.

  1. Midpoints: 5, 15, 25
  2. Σfx = 4 × 5 + 10 × 15 + 6 × 25 = 20 + 150 + 150 = 320
  3. Mean = 320 ÷ 20

Answer: 16 cm

Find the median of 8, 3, 11, 5, 9, 2.

Show answer
  1. Order: 2, 3, 5, 8, 9, 11
  2. Middle two: 5 and 8

Answer: 6.5

Q2Warm-up

Find the mode and the range of 4, 7, 7, 2, 9, 7, 3.

Show answer
  1. 7 appears 3 times
  2. Range = 9 − 2

Answer: mode 7, range 7

Q3Standard

The mean of 5 numbers is 12. Four of them are 10, 14, 9 and 15. Find the fifth number.

Show answer
  1. Total = 5 × 12 = 60
  2. 60 − (10 + 14 + 9 + 15) = 60 − 48

Answer: 12

Q4Standard

  3. 290 ÷ 20

  1. n = 20, so the median is between the 10th and 11th values
  2. First class has 6, first two classes have 15

Q6Exam-style

The mean of 10 numbers is 7. One number is removed and the mean of the other 9 is 6.5. Which number was removed?

Show answer
  1. Total of 10 = 70
  2. Total of 9 = 58.5
  3. 70 − 58.5

Answer: 11.5
