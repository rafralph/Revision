How the data was collected

All question papers came from AQA's own file store: Biology 8461, Chemistry 8462 and Physics 8463, papers 1H and 2H, from June 2018 to June 2023. Some papers could not be opened, and one Biology question (June 2018, question 6) was withdrawn by AQA, so it is not counted. AQA has not released the 2024 and 2025 papers to the public yet.

Each sub-question went into one topic. Science questions often mix topics, so treat the percentages as a guide to priority, not exact figures.
