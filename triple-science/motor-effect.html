Magnetism and the motor effect

Physics · rank 7 of 29 · 48 marks · 4.8% of Physics marks · in 6 of 10 papers

VideoExplanationsDiagramsWorked examplesPractice

Explanations 4 ways

1The picture idea

A current in a magnetic field feels a push. Your left hand tells you the direction: thumb for motion, first finger for field, second finger for current.

2Step by step

Like poles repel. Unlike poles attract. Magnetic field lines go from north to south. A permanent magnet has its own field. An induced magnet becomes a magnet only in a field.

A current in a wire makes a magnetic field. A solenoid (a coil) concentrates the field. An iron core makes an electromagnet stronger.

Motor effect: a wire carrying a current in a magnetic field feels a force. F = BIl (B in tesla, I in amps, l in metres).

Fleming's left-hand rule: thumb = thrust (force), first finger = field, second finger = current.

In an electric motor, a split-ring commutator reverses the current every half turn, so the coil keeps turning the same way.

CAUTIONIf the wire is parallel to the field, there is no force.

3Another way to see it

A wire carrying a current has its own magnetic field. Put it between two magnets and the two fields push on each other, so the wire is pushed. This is the motor effect.

Use Fleming's left-hand rule to find the direction: thuMb = Motion (force), First finger = Field (N → S), seCond finger = Current.

Size of the force: F = B I l (flux density × current × length of wire in the field).

In a motor, the force pushes one side of a coil up and the other down, so it spins. A loudspeaker uses a changing current to make a cone vibrate.

4Watch out for

  • Using the right hand. The motor effect uses the left hand.
  • Getting the field direction wrong. Field lines go from N to S.
  • Forgetting to change cm to m for the length in F = BIl.
  • Saying there is a force when the wire is parallel to the field. There is none.
  • Saying permanent magnets need a current.

Worked examples 3

Example 1 Warm-up

In which direction do magnetic field lines point outside a magnet?

    Answer: From the north pole to the south pole

    Example 2 Standard

    A 0.10 m wire carries 2.5 A at right angles to a 0.40 T field. Calculate the force.

    1. F = BIl = 0.40 × 2.5 × 0.10

    Answer: 0.10 N

    Example 3 Exam-style [3 marks]

    A wire 5.0 cm long carries a current of 4.0 A at right angles to a magnetic field of 0.25 T. Calculate the force on the wire.

    1. l = 5.0 cm = 0.050 m
    2. F = B × I × l = 0.25 × 4.0 × 0.050
    3. = 0.05 N

    Answer: 0.05 N

    Q2Warm-up

    Show answer

    Answer: A material that becomes a magnet only when it is in a magnetic field

    Q3Standard

    Give three ways to make an electromagnet stronger.

    Show answer

    Answer: More current, more turns, an iron core

    Q4Standard

    F = 0.060 N, I = 3.0 A, l = 0.050 m. Calculate the flux density B.

    Show answer
    1. B = F ÷ (Il) = 0.060 ÷ (3.0 × 0.050)

    Answer: 0.40 T

    Q5Exam-style

    Explain how a loudspeaker makes sound.

    Show answer
    1. An alternating current flows in a coil in a magnetic field.
    2. The motor effect pushes the coil back and forth.
    3. The coil moves a cone, which makes the air vibrate (sound waves).

    Answer: Changing current → changing force on coil → cone vibrates → sound

    Q6Exam-style

    Give three ways to make an electric motor spin faster.

    Show answer

    Answer: Increase the current, use a stronger magnet, use more turns on the coil
