Generators, transformers and induction

Physics · rank 3 of 29 · 56 marks · 5.6% of Physics marks · in 5 of 10 papers

VideoExplanationsDiagramsWorked examplesPractice

Explanations 4 ways

1The picture idea

Move a wire through a magnetic field, or change the field around a coil, and a potential difference appears. Transformers change the field all the time with alternating current.

2Step by step

The generator effect: when a conductor cuts magnetic field lines, a potential difference is induced. If the circuit is complete, a current flows. The induced current makes its own magnetic field, which opposes the change.

An alternator makes a.c. It uses slip rings. A dynamo makes d.c. It uses a split-ring commutator. Microphones use the generator effect. Loudspeakers use the motor effect.

A transformer has two coils on an iron core. An alternating current in the primary coil makes a changing magnetic field in the core. This induces an alternating p.d. in the secondary coil.

Vₚ ÷ Vₛ = nₚ ÷ nₛ. For a 100% efficient transformer, Vₚ × Iₚ = Vₛ × Iₛ.

A step-up transformer has more turns on the secondary coil. The National Grid steps up the p.d., so the current is small and less energy is lost as heat.

CAUTIONTransformers only work with a.c. A direct current gives no changing field.

3Another way to see it

Move a magnet in and out of a coil of wire and a p.d. is induced, like stirring electrons with a magnetic spoon. This is the generator effect. Get a bigger p.d. by moving faster, using a stronger magnet or adding more turns.

An alternator makes AC (it uses slip rings). A dynamo makes DC (it uses a split-ring commutator).

A transformer changes the size of an alternating p.d., like gears change speed. More turns on the secondary coil → higher p.d. (step-up). The national grid steps up to very high p.d. so the current is low and less energy is lost as heat in the cables.

Vp ÷ Vs = np ÷ ns and, for a perfect transformer, VpIp = VsIs.

4Watch out for

  • Saying transformers work with DC. They need AC (a changing magnetic field).
  • Saying a step-up transformer increases power. Power stays the same (current falls).
  • Saying high voltage on the grid is for speed. It is to reduce current and heat loss.
  • Putting the turns ratio upside down.
  • Saying a magnet held still in a coil induces a p.d. It must move (or the field must change).

Worked examples 3

Example 1 Warm-up

What does a step-up transformer do?

    Answer: It increases the p.d. (it has more turns on the secondary coil)

    Example 2 Standard

    A transformer has Vp = 230 V and Ip = 0.50 A. Vs = 11.5 V. Calculate Is.

    1. VpIp = VsIs
    2. 230 × 0.50 = 11.5 × Is
    3. Is = 115 ÷ 11.5

    Answer: 10 A

    Example 3 Exam-style [4 marks]

    A transformer has 2000 turns on the primary coil and 100 turns on the secondary coil. The primary p.d. is 230 V.
    (a) Calculate the secondary p.d. (b) The secondary current is 2.0 A. Calculate the primary current. Assume 100% efficiency.

    1. (a) Vₛ = Vₚ × nₛ ÷ nₚ = 230 × 100 ÷ 2000
    2. = 11.5 V
    3. (b) Vₚ × Iₚ = Vₛ × Iₛ, so 230 × Iₚ = 11.5 × 2.0 = 23 W
    4. Iₚ = 23 ÷ 230

    Answer: (a) 11.5 V; (b) 0.10 A

    Practice 6 questions · easy to exam-style

    Try each one first. Then open the answer to check your working.

    Q1Warm-up

    Why does the national grid transmit electricity at a very high p.d.?

    Show answer

    Answer: So the current is low, which reduces energy lost as heat in the cables

    Q2Warm-up

    Which makes AC: an alternator or a dynamo?

    Show answer

    Answer: An alternator

    Q3Standard

    A transformer has 100 primary turns and 500 secondary turns. The primary p.d. is 12 V. Calculate the secondary p.d.

    Show answer
    1. Vs = 12 × 500 ÷ 100

    Answer: 60 V

    Q4Standard

    Give three ways to increase the p.d. induced in a coil.

    Show answer

    Answer: Move the magnet faster, use a stronger magnet, use more turns

    Q5Exam-style

    A transformer has 400 primary turns and 20 secondary turns. Vp = 240 V and Is = 2.0 A. Calculate Vs and Ip.

    Show answer
    1. Vs = 240 × 20 ÷ 400 = 12 V
    2. Ip = VsIs ÷ Vp = 24 ÷ 240

    Answer: 12 V and 0.10 A

    Q6Exam-style

    Explain how a moving-coil microphone works.

    Show answer
    1. Sound waves make the diaphragm vibrate.
    2. The coil attached to it moves in a magnetic field.
    3. A p.d. is induced that matches the sound wave.

    Answer: Sound → diaphragm and coil move in a magnetic field → varying induced p.d.
