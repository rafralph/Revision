Heart, blood, vessels and lungs

Biology · rank 3 of 31 · 56 marks · 6.3% of Biology marks · in 4 of 9 papers

Explanations 4 ways

1The picture idea

Blood goes through the heart twice on each full lap: once to the lungs to collect oxygen, and once to the body to deliver it.

2Step by step

The heart pumps blood around the body in a double circulation. AQA tests the route through the heart, blood vessels, blood components and the lungs.

  1. Deoxygenated blood enters the right atrium from the vena cava.
  2. The right ventricle pumps it to the lungs in the pulmonary artery.
  3. Oxygenated blood returns to the left atrium in the pulmonary vein.
  4. The left ventricle pumps it to the body in the aorta. Its wall is thicker because it pumps blood further.
  5. Valves stop blood flowing backwards.

Arteries have thick, elastic walls for high pressure. Veins have valves. Capillaries are one cell thick for exchange.

CAUTIONArteries carry blood away from the heart. The pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood.

3Another way to see it

The heart is two pumps side by side. The right side pumps blood to the lungs to pick up oxygen. The left side pumps it to the rest of the body. This is a double circulation: blood goes through the heart twice on each trip.

The left ventricle has a thicker wall because it pushes blood all round the body.

  • Arteries carry blood away from the heart: thick, stretchy walls for high pressure.
  • Veins bring blood back: thinner walls and valves to stop backflow.
  • Capillaries: walls one cell thick, so substances diffuse in and out quickly.

4Watch out for

  • Swapping left and right. On a diagram, the heart's left is on your right.
  • Saying arteries always carry oxygenated blood. The pulmonary artery carries deoxygenated blood to the lungs.
  • Saying arteries have valves. Veins (and the heart) have valves.
  • Saying red blood cells have a nucleus. They do not: more room for haemoglobin.
  • Saying capillaries carry blood to the heart.

Worked examples 3

Example 1 Warm-up

Which type of blood vessel carries blood away from the heart?

    Answer: Arteries

    Example 2 Standard

    Explain why the wall of the left ventricle is thicker than the right.

    1. The left ventricle pumps blood all around the body.
    2. This needs a higher pressure than pumping to the lungs.

    Answer: It must pump blood at high pressure all round the body

    Example 3 Exam-style [4 marks]

    Describe the route taken by blood from the vena cava to the aorta.

    1. Vena cava → right atrium → right ventricle
    2. → pulmonary artery → lungs (blood is oxygenated)
    3. → pulmonary vein → left atrium → left ventricle
    4. → aorta

    Answer: Right atrium → right ventricle → lungs → left atrium → left ventricle → aorta

    Q5Exam-style

    Explain why a double circulation is an advantage to mammals.

    Show answer
    1. Blood returns to the heart after the lungs.
    2. It is pumped again at high pressure.
    3. So oxygen reaches the body cells quickly.

    Answer: Blood is re-pumped after the lungs, so it reaches the body at high pressure and fast

    Q6Exam-style

    Describe how the alveoli are adapted for gas exchange.

    Show answer

    Answer: Huge surface area; walls one cell thick (short diffusion distance); good blood supply from capillaries; ventilation keeps a steep concentration gradient
